# Style seeds

Use a seed only when the brief lacks a concrete visual direction. Seeds are
starting vocabulary, not replacement palettes for TinyRoute's `DESIGN.md`.

## 1. Cream editorial

- Mood: warm, literary, quiet, tactile
- Typical structure: serif-led hierarchy, narrow reading measure, large margins
- Motion: minimal fades and link transitions
- Risk: generic beige luxury styling; use only when the brief supports it

## 2. Dark technical

- Mood: precise, focused, tool-like, nocturnal
- Typical structure: near-black surfaces, compact controls, sharp information
  hierarchy
- Motion: subtle state transitions and carefully bounded reveals
- Risk: neon glow, purple gradients, and decorative terminal clichés

## 3. Minimal neutral

- Mood: calm, exact, gallery-like
- Typical structure: strong typography, open space, quiet dividers
- Motion: L1-level feedback only
- Risk: becoming empty or generic instead of intentionally minimal

## 4. Warm professional

- Mood: trustworthy, approachable, mature
- Typical structure: clear proof, balanced spacing, rounded but disciplined UI
- Motion: subtle lift, opacity, and state changes
- Risk: default blue SaaS visuals without a distinct product voice

## 5. Playful creative

- Mood: energetic, surprising, youthful
- Typical structure: asymmetry, bold scale changes, expressive illustration
- Motion: one or two purposeful signature interactions
- Risk: reduced readability, excessive color, and novelty without hierarchy

## 6. East Asian editorial

- Mood: restrained, literary, spacious
- Typical structure: deliberate line length, calm rhythm, language-appropriate
  typography such as Noto Serif SC or Noto Sans SC when Chinese content exists
- Motion: slow fades at section or line granularity
- Risk: treating diverse cultural aesthetics as a single decorative trope

## 7. Cyberpunk

- Mood: high-contrast, underground, synthetic
- Typical structure: angular grids, monospace accents, controlled signal colors
- Motion: glitch or scanning effects used sparingly
- Risk: poor accessibility, visual noise, and continuous GPU-heavy animation

## 8. Organic natural

- Mood: tactile, sustainable, soft, handmade
- Typical structure: irregular but readable composition, material imagery,
  softened geometry
- Motion: gentle, low-amplitude transitions
- Risk: interchangeable craft-brand beige and green

## 9. Swiss grid

- Mood: rational, direct, typographic
- Typical structure: strict grid, asymmetric alignment, high-contrast type
- Motion: functional transitions only
- Risk: rigid layouts that fail to adapt on small screens

## 10. Glass surfaces

- Mood: layered, luminous, spatial
- Typical structure: translucent surfaces over a simple background hierarchy
- Motion: restrained depth and opacity transitions
- Risk: low contrast, expensive blur, and glass applied to every container

## Combining seeds

Mix at most two compatible aspects: for example, dark-technical color roles with
minimal-neutral motion, or Swiss-grid composition with warm-professional tone.
Keep one palette, one typography system, and one shape system. Never use a seed
to override established TinyRoute tokens without an explicit design-system task.
